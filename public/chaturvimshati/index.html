Chronicle
  • The board is 3 rows × 8 columns — twenty-four squares (चतुर्विंशति कोष्ठक), a game from the Sanskrit text Krīḍākauśalya.
  • Each player commands 8 pieces: ivory hold the top row, crimson the bottom; the middle row starts empty.
  • Move one piece one square horizontally or vertically into an empty square.
  • Capture by jumping over an adjacent enemy piece into the empty square immediately beyond it — the jumped piece is removed.
  • If more jumps are available, the same piece keeps capturing in one turn.
  • Win by capturing every enemy piece, or by leaving the enemy with no legal move.
