Chronicle
  • 3 tigers start on the apex triangle; 15 goats start off the board.
  • The goat player moves first, placing one goat per turn on any empty point until all 15 are placed.
  • Once placed, a goat moves one step along a line each turn.
  • A tiger moves to an adjacent empty point, or captures by jumping over an adjacent goat to the empty point straight beyond it — the eaten goat leaves the board.
  • ⚠️ A goat placed or moved into a tiger's jaws is eaten immediately.
  • Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. Goats win by surrounding the tigers so none can move.
