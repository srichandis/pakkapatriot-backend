Chronicle
  • Vish (🕷️ the poison) hunts three runners (🏃) across a 7×7 board.
  • Runners move one square (any of 8 directions). Vish can move one square — or leap two squares in a straight line, outrunning them.
  • If Vish touches a runner, that runner freezes into a Statue (🗿) — frozen stone, unable to move.
  • A free runner who ends their move touching a statue cries “Amrit!” and releases them back into the chase.
  • Vish wins by freezing all three runners. The runners win by surviving 60 rounds without being fully frozen.
