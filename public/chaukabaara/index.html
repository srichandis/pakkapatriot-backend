The Cowries
Chronicle
  • Throw 4 cowrie shells. Mouths-up count 1, 2, 3; all mouths = Chamma (4); all humps = Ashta (8).
  • Chamma & Ashta grant a bonus throw. Three in a row — the whole turn is forfeited!
  • Every pawn starts already on the board, stacked on its own home square — nothing to enter.
  • Pieces travel anti-clockwise around the outer ring, then clockwise through the inner squares to the centre.
  • Move only into the inner squares after you have captured at least one opponent piece.
  • Landing exactly on a lone opponent piece captures it — it returns to its home square (extra throw!). Landing on an ✕ safe square protects you.
  • Two of your pieces on one square form a double: it blocks each opponent once, cannot be captured by a single piece, and may be moved together.
  • The centre must be reached by exact count. First to bring all pieces home wins.
